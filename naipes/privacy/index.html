Privacy policy — Naipes

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Naipes is a card game. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no email address and no profile. We do not know who you are, and nothing in this app is designed to find out.

This policy describes what the app collects, why, and how to stop it. It is written to be read rather than to be complied with, but it is also the notice required by the GDPR and by the Google Play and App Store data disclosures.

The short version

Advertising To keep the app free Ads: no. Personalised ads: yes

You are asked about the first two before you see a card, and you can change both at any time from Privacy on the menu.

What we collect

Anonymous usage events — only if you agree

If you say yes to "help improve the game", the app records what happens in it: which game you opened, which house rules you chose, how a hand ended, where you stopped, and when you tapped a card the rules would not allow.

There is a specific reason this matters more than usual for this app. Spanish card games are played differently in every household, and the arguments are about the rules. When somebody repeatedly taps a card our engine forbids, that is somebody telling us our rules are wrong — and it is the only way we will ever find out. It is why this exists.

These events carry no name, no email, no phone number, no advertising identifier and no device identifier. They carry the app version, the platform, your language and region, how many hands you have played in total, and the rule settings you chose.

The events are processed by PostHog, on servers in the European Union.

If you say no, nothing is sent. The app works identically.

Advertising

The free version shows an advert at the end of a hand, at most one every three hands, and never during play. Adverts are served by Google AdMob.

You are asked separately whether you want personalised adverts. If you say no, you will still see adverts, but non-personalised ones — Google is told to serve them without using an advertising profile. Refusing personalisation does not remove advertising; buying Naipes Completo does, permanently.

Google's own handling of advertising data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

If you buy Naipes Completo, the purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store, and validated through RevenueCat. We never see and never store your card details, your name or your billing address — the store does not give them to us. What we receive is an anonymous identifier and the fact that a purchase exists, so that the app can restore it if you reinstall or change phone.

Your game record

Hands, matches, draws and your best run are kept on your phone only. They are never uploaded, never sent to us and never visible to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we never do

  • We never ask for, collect or store your name, email address or phone number.
  • We never sell data to anybody, for any purpose.
  • We never track you across other apps or websites.
  • We never send your game record anywhere.
  • There is no account, so there is nothing to be breached.

Children

Naipes is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. The games are traditional adult card games with no gambling, no real money wagering and no chat.

Your rights

Because we hold nothing that identifies you, we cannot look up "your" data — there is no key to look it up by. What you can do at any time:

  • Turn analytics off — Privacy on the menu. Nothing further is sent.
  • Turn off personalisation — the same screen.
  • Delete everything — uninstalling the app removes your record and settings from the device. Events already sent are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you, by us or by anyone else.

Under the GDPR the legal basis for analytics and personalised advertising is your consent, which you give or refuse on the first screen and can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised advertising and purchase validation rest on legitimate interest and on performing the contract you entered into when you bought something.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the app will ask you again rather than quietly assuming your old answer still applies.
